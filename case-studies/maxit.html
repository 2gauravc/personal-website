Case study · Maxit

AI-powered banking intelligence for better context and decision support.

Maxit is a concept for a banking intelligence system that helps relationship managers, product teams, and bankers work with richer client context, clearer reasoning, and stronger decision preparation.

Problem Why work breaks Proposed solution Agentic capabilities Lessons learned

Problem

Banking teams often work without a reliable shared picture.

Relationship managers and product specialists need to understand client activity, risk signals, internal history, product fit, and open decisions. That context is usually spread across systems, documents, inboxes, meeting notes, and individual memory.

Why current knowledge work breaks

The issue is not only information access. It is decision flow.

Teams spend too much effort reconstructing context before they can make progress. Handovers lose nuance, internal knowledge is hard to retrieve, and evidence is rarely packaged in a way that supports a clear next action.

Proposed solution

A banking intelligence layer that prepares the work.

Maxit would connect approved internal and external sources, summarize client context, identify relevant history, prepare briefing notes, and help teams reason through relationship, product, and risk questions before meetings or decisions.

Agentic capabilities

Agents can gather, compare, draft, and escalate.

  • Client context retrieval across approved systems and documents.
  • Meeting and opportunity preparation with evidence-backed summaries.
  • Product and coverage prompts based on client needs and constraints.
  • Decision support that separates facts, assumptions, gaps, and recommendations.
  • Human review controls for sensitive outputs and regulated decisions.

Lessons learned

Useful AI starts with workflow design.

The core lesson is that intelligence tools must fit how banking decisions actually move. Retrieval, summarization, and agents matter most when they improve the rhythm of preparation, review, escalation, and action.
