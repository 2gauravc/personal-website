About

Banking transformation, AI systems, and practical enterprise adoption.

I work at the intersection of banking transformation, agentic systems, and knowledge work redesign. This site collects case studies, frameworks, and notes on making AI useful inside complex regulated organizations.

Profile

A builder’s view of banking change.

I approach AI adoption from the operating reality of banks: fragmented systems, high control requirements, sensitive client data, legacy processes, and teams that carry large amounts of institutional knowledge in meetings, inboxes, and documents.

My focus is practical transformation rather than AI hype. That means looking at where knowledge work breaks, how better shared context changes decisions, and where agentic systems can support humans without weakening accountability.

Alongside banking transformation experience, I bring hands-on cloud and software practice. That combination helps connect strategy, architecture, workflow design, and execution.

Banking transformation

Agentic systems

Exploring how AI agents can gather context, use tools, reason through options, and hand work back to humans.

Knowledge work redesign

Improving how teams make decisions, coordinate expertise, and move complex work through governed processes.
