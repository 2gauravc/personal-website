State of Knowledge Work in Banking

Before diving into agents and copilots, we have to acknowledge the reality of knowledge work in banks. Repetitive low-value work, overloaded teams and people operating under constant stress - just to keep daily operations moving.

Besides Agentic AI, there is so much more to fix: functional silos, fragmented processes, legacy technology, overloaded delivery teams, and evolving people skills. So where do we even begin?

Since this moment is about Agentic AI, let us start there. And since transformation ultimately begins with people, let us begin with YOU!
