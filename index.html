Rethinking Knowledge Work

The future of work is not simply about automating tasks. It is about redesigning how knowledge, decisions, technology, and people work together. Through ideas, experiments, and real-world enterprise challenges, explore what this shift means - for banking and beyond.

Think

Why is knowledge work still manual, fragmented, and difficult to scale? Why do enterprise systems become complex over time? Why does enterprise transformation progress so much slower than the underlying technology?

Build

Ideas become useful only when tested against reality. Designing and building practical AI systems, agentic workflows, and enterprise solutions to explore how intelligence can be embedded into real work.

Teach

The best way to deepen understanding is to explain it. Sharing frameworks, lessons, successes, and failures from the journey of applying technology to solve meaningful business problems.
