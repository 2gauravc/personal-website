01 / Current Reality

Perennial Resource Crunch

Organizations operate under constant resource pressure. Work remains fragmented across specialized teams and systems - a modern version of the manufacturing assembly line. Technology has helped automate individual tasks at scale, but often reinforces fragmentation. Technology delivery itself is optimized for predictability, making simple changes expensive and slowing the organization's ability to adapt.

02 / Current Reality

High Operational Toil

Technology has automated many tasks, but not the work around them. Knowledge workers still spend much of their day gathering information, rebuilding context, and coordinating across fragmented processes.

03 / Current Reality

Wheels Grind Slow

Things move slowly as teams navigate long-winded processes, dependencies, and coordination overhead. Operational learning is repeatedly lost and rediscovered, making every effort feel like a first time. Relentless effort; incremental returns.

04 / Current Reality

Overworked and Unhappy People

Every day is a grind. Days stay full - often with repetitive low value work, energy drains quickly, and work feels exhausting rather than meaningful.
