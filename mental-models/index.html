Mental models

From external shifts to concrete actions.

Agentic AI is not just another tooling wave. The useful question for banks is how the world is shifting, and what practical operating model, technology, delivery, and people moves can create capacity instead of more complexity.

Shifts Actions

01 / Shifts

Where the pressure is coming from.

Start with the forces already visible: resource crunch, operational toil, agentic technology, and the pull toward work happening closer to the people who understand it.

The leaf nodes in the diagram are the things to inspect. They are the signals that explain why old delivery models feel slower than the world around them.

02 / Actions

What banks can do about it.

The response is not a single AI initiative. It is a coordinated set of moves across work design, technology foundations, delivery capability, and people roles.

The action map turns the shift into a practical agenda: improve decision flow, modernize foundations, reshape delivery, and prepare domain practitioners, platform builders, and digital guardians for new responsibilities.
